What the data cannot tell us

A small slice. A precise question.

Own timetables

Matching route, date and departure window does not align arrival deadlines. Scheduled buffers and minute-level timing remain bundled with fleet, staffing, rotations and operating circumstances. The result measures timetable-promise performance, not common-clock speed or a cause.

United Express excluded

Only United-operated and Southwest-operated flights enter the contrast. Other operators flying under United’s brand are excluded. This is not a brand-wide quality ranking, connection-success measure or passenger-weighted comparison.

Small overlap

Comparable coverage is __COVER_UA__ of United-operated schedules and __COVER_WN__ of Southwest-operated schedules in A / B. A and B have separate frozen baskets. The estimate does not describe the unserved overlap or the whole network.

Denver-heavy

Denver touches __DENVER__ of comparison weight in A / B. Airport shares cannot be added: both endpoints can touch the same flight. Airport-omission and time-band lenses show why one average is not a universal advantage.

Heterogeneity remains

Early and late local departure bands differ substantially. Time-of-day checks are post-hoc, with no computed band intervals. A positive pooled result does not establish an advantage in every month, airport or time band.

Not a recommendation

These data do not identify the effect of choosing an airline, safety, fares, rebooking or eventual passenger arrival. This page is not airline-choice advice or a guarantee for a future trip.
